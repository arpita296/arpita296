আমাদের গল্প · Our Story

Where Bengal's
Clay Speaks

"মাটির তৈরি পাত্রে রান্না করা খাবারের স্বাদই আলাদা"
"Food cooked in earthen vessels carries a taste that is uniquely its own"
— Bengali Kitchen Wisdom

Terracotta takes its name and soul from Bengal's most ancient craft — the fired clay that shaped civilisation. Like the terracotta temples of Bishnupur, carved with stories of life and devotion, our kitchen tells its own story through flavour. Vegetarian and Non-Vegetarian multi-cuisine delicacies, served with the warmth of a Bengali household and the refinement of a fine dining lounge.

Nestled in Matigara, Siliguri — where the Terai meets the hills — we are a restaurant and lounge for families, friends, and anyone who believes a great meal is its own poem.

অভিজ্ঞতা · The Experience

The Art of Dining

মাটি

Terracotta Ambience

পোড়ামাটির পরিবেশ

Step into walls carved with Bengal's finest terracotta reliefs — scenes from mythology, nature and daily life — just like the ancient temples of Bishnupur. Dining here is an immersion in living heritage.

রান্না

Multi-Cuisine Kitchen

বহু-রান্নার স্বাদ

From the spiced gravies of Bengal to Mughlai richness, South Indian freshness to Continental elegance — our kitchen spans civilisations. Both vegetarian and non-vegetarian, crafted with care in every dish.

অসীম

All You Can Eat

সীমাহীন ভোজন

Terracotta always offers the rare pleasure of an unlimited spread. Come hungry, bring your whole family. Eat without restraint — the Bengali way. This is how we celebrate food: joyfully, abundantly.
